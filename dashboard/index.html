        What this analysis can—and cannot—answer.

        Use one aggregation level.

        Manufacturer rows repeat parts of the Overall summaries. The pipeline keeps Overall rows only, at brand + ingredient + year.

        Keep the population in view.

        These are Medicare Part D products. The findings do not estimate a specific employer’s costs. Beneficiaries can appear under several drugs and are not added into a unique-person total.

        Separate spending from price.

        Average spending per fill reflects days supplied and product mix. It is not a standardized price, and the dataset does not reveal net cost after rebates.

        A current question, with a visible data lag.

        Pharmacy costs remain a concern in the 2026 employer survey. This analysis uses CMS’s June 2026 release, covering 2024 and its historical columns. The selected cohort includes single-agent GLP-1 drugs and tirzepatide; insulin combinations are excluded. Treatment indication, clinical effectiveness, and current-year spend cannot be inferred here.
